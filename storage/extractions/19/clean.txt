SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 Objective
This policy establishes the mandatory security rules, access governance protocols, data protection standards, and acceptable technology use guidelines across all regional offices of Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Delhi, Mumbai, and Singapore).

1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees, probationary staff, fixed-term contractors, third-party vendors, and systems managed by or connected to Nexora’s global network infrastructure.

SECTION 2: IDENTITY, PASSWORDS, AND ACCOUNT SECURITY

2.1 Credential Standards (Policy v3.0 Active Standard)
- Minimum Length: 16 characters.
- Complexity Rules: Must contain at least one uppercase letter [A-Z], one lowercase letter [a-z], one numeric digit [0-9], and one non-alphanumeric symbol [e.g., !, @, #, $, %, ^, &, *].
- Historical Recycling Block: Users cannot reuse any of their last 12 previously used passwords.
- Expiration Lifecycle: Automated forced password reset occurs every 90 days.
- Account Lockout Threshold: Five (5) consecutive failed authentication attempts will lock the Active Directory / Okta account for 30 minutes. Unlocking prior to 30 minutes requires identity verification via the IT Helpdesk Lead Tarun Bajaj (EMP-1455).

2.2 Hardware-Backed Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Primary MFA Token: YubiKey 5 Series NFC hardware token issued during IT onboarding.
- Secondary MFA Token: Okta Verify Application with Number Matching enabled.
- SMS-based authentication is strictly deprecated and disabled due to SIM-swapping vulnerabilities.

SECTION 3: DEVICE & ENDPOINT SECURITY

3.1 Full-Disk Encryption & Endpoint Protection
- All corporate laptops (MacBook Pro / Lenovo ThinkPad / Dell XPS) must maintain 100% full-disk encryption (FileVault 2 on macOS; BitLocker with TPM 2.0 on Windows).
- Nexora Secure Endpoint Agent (PRD-102) and CrowdStrike Falcon must remain active at all times. Tampering with or disabling security daemons is a Level 3 disciplinary infraction.

3.2 Removable Media Block
- USB mass storage drives, external hard drives, and SD cards are blocked at the kernel level via MDM profile restrictions.
- Any business requirement for external drive access must be approved by CISO Karthik Natarajan (EMP-1072) and submitted via a formal IT Exception Ticket.

SECTION 4: NETWORK SECURITY & REMOTE WORK PROTOCOLS

4.1 Remote Work & Telecommuting Security Procedures
When working remotely (under the 3/2 Hybrid Model detailed in DOC-NHB-001):
- VPN Requirement: Remote connections to Nexora’s production environment, code repositories (GitHub Enterprise), or internal portals must route through Palo Alto GlobalProtect VPN (`vpn-in.nexora.com` or `vpn-sg.nexora.com`).
- Public Wi-Fi Restriction: Employees are strictly forbidden from connecting corporate assets to unencrypted public Wi-Fi networks (e.g., airport, cafe, hotel Wi-Fi) unless the GlobalProtect VPN tunnel is actively established first.
- Physical Screen Privacy: When working in public spaces, privacy screen filters are required. Devices must never be left unattended.
- Geographical Commute Limit: Employees must remain within a 2-hour commute radius of their base office. Remote work outside their registered country of employment without approval from Head of HR Rahul Desai (EMP-1001) and Legal Counsel Neha Gupta (EMP-1065) is prohibited.

4.2 Stolen or Lost Laptop Protocol
If a corporate device is lost or stolen:
1. Immediately notify the IT Security Hotline (+91-80-NEXORA-SEC) or email `security@nexora.com` within two (2) hours of discovery.
2. Sr. Systems Administrator Tarun Bajaj (EMP-1455) will issue a remote wipe command via Jamf/Intune to erase all local encryption keys and data.
3. Incident Response Lead Manish Pandey (EMP-1351) will isolate the user's Active Directory and SaaS identity sessions.

SECTION 6: SECURITY AWARENESS & VIOLATIONS

6.1 Phishing Simulations & Training
- All employees undergo unannounced monthly phishing simulation tests managed by Sr. Security Analyst Kiran Patel (EMP-1350).
- Any employee who fails a phishing simulation (e.g., clicks a test malicious link or enters credentials) is automatically enrolled in a mandatory 2-hour Remedial Cyber Security Training module.
- Failing three consecutive phishing simulations within a 12-month period triggers an escalation to HRBP Aisha Khan (EMP-1402) and formal disciplinary review.

6.2 Policy Violation Governance
Violations of this Information Security Policy (such as credential sharing, disabling EDR agents, or unauthorized data exfiltration) are subject to progressive disciplinary actions under DOC-NHR-005, up to and including immediate termination for cause, forfeiture of unpaid bonuses, and potential legal prosecution under statutory computer misuse laws.
