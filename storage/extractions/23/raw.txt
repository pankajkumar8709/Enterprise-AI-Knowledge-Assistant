SECTION 1: HARDWARE PROVISIONING & LAPTOP ALLOCATION

1.1 Standard Hardware Allocation Profiles
All new joiners at Nexora Technologies receive company-owned hardware based on their job role profile. Hardware allocation is managed by IT Systems Administrator Tarun Bajaj (EMP-1455) under the supervision of IT Head Amit Patel (EMP-1015).

- Profile A (Engineering, R&D, Cybersecurity):
  * Hardware: Apple MacBook Pro 16" (M3 Pro, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD) OR Dell XPS 15 (Intel i9, 64GB RAM, 1TB SSD, NVIDIA RTX 4060).
  * Accessories: Dual 27" 4K Monitors, USB-C Docking Station, YubiKey 5C NFC Security Token.
- Profile B (Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations, Legal, Procurement):
  * Hardware: Lenovo ThinkPad T14s (Intel i7, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) OR Apple MacBook Air 15" (M3, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD).
  * Accessories: Single 27" Monitor, USB-C Multi-port Adapter, YubiKey 5 NFC Token.

1.2 Laptop Offboarding & Asset Refresh Cycle
- Asset Refresh Cycle: Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Old laptops are securely wiped using DoD 5220.22-M standards by Procurement Specialist Ramesh Babu (EMP-1499).
- Offboarding Protocol: On an employee's Last Working Day (LWD), all physical hardware must be surrendered to the local IT desk by 4:00 PM local time.

SECTION 6: TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON IT ISSUES

6.1 Issue: VPN Failed to Establish Security Association
- Symptoms: GlobalProtect client stuck at 94% "Connecting".
- Root Cause: Stale RADIUS token session or local network UDP port 4500 blocked.
- Resolution Steps:
  1. Open terminal and run: `sudo launchctl stop com.paloaltonetworks.gp.daemon`
  2. Clear local DNS cache: `sudo dscacheutil -flushcache`
  3. Restart GlobalProtect client and re-authenticate via Okta MFA.

6.2 Issue: YubiKey Hardware Token Not Recognized
- Resolution: Insert key, open Self-Service Portal, execute "Re-sync Cryptographic Token", and press YubiKey gold contact button for 3 seconds when prompted.
