1. POLICY PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy defines the rules, entitlements, application workflows, carry-forward limits, encashment parameters, and attendance regulations governing all full-time regular and probationary employees of Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. across all office locations.

3. DETAILED LEAVE CATEGORIES AND RULES

3.1 Annual Leave (AL)
- Quota: 24 working days per calendar year accrued at 2.0 days per completed calendar month.
- Application Window: Requests for 1 to 3 continuous AL days must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance. Requests for 4 or more continuous AL days require 15 calendar days advance notice.
- Max Continuous Duration: Employees cannot take more than 10 continuous AL working days without explicit approval from their Department Head.
- Accrual during Probation: Probationary employees accrue AL at 2.0 days/month but can only utilize accrued AL after successful completion of 3 months of service.

3.2 Sick Leave (SL)
- Quota: 12 calendar days per year accrued at 1.0 day per completed month.
- Notification: Employees must notify their manager via Slack/Email before 9:30 AM on the day of absence.
- Medical Certificate Requirement: Any SL extending beyond 3 consecutive working days requires a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner uploaded to the HRMS upon returning to work.
- Conversion of AL to SL: If an employee falls seriously ill while on approved Annual Leave, the affected days can be converted to Sick Leave upon submission of medical proof within 48 hours of resume.

3.3 Casual Leave (CL)
- Quota: 8 days per year, fully credited on January 1st (or prorated for mid-year joiners).
- Purpose: Intended for unexpected personal matters, home emergencies, or personal administrative work.
- Restrictions: Casual leave cannot be combined with Annual Leave. A maximum of 2 consecutive CL days can be taken at any one time.

3.4 Maternity Leave
- Entitlement: 26 weeks (182 calendar days) of fully paid maternity leave as mandated by law.
- Eligibility: Female employees who have worked at Nexora for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.
- Extension: Up to 4 additional weeks of unpaid leave can be granted upon submission of medical documentation showing health complications.

3.5 Paternity Leave
- Entitlement: 4 weeks (20 working days) of fully paid paternity leave for male employees upon the birth or adoption of a child.
- Usage Window: Must be utilized within 6 months of the child's birth/adoption in maximums of two distinct two-week blocks.

3.6 Leave Without Pay (LWP) / Unpaid Leave
- Conditions: Applicable only when all paid leave balances (AL, SL, CL) have been completely exhausted.
- Capping: Maximum limit of 30 cumulative days of LWP per calendar year.
- Approval Path: Requires written approval from Manager, Department Head, and Head of HR (Rahul Desai, EMP-1001).
- Payroll Impact: Pro-rata deduction of gross daily salary for each day on LWP.

3.7 Emergency / Bereavement Leave
- Entitlement: Up to 3 fully paid days in the event of the death or critical medical emergency of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Does not deduct from AL/SL/CL balances.

4. LEAVE CARRY-FORWARD AND ENCASHMENT RULES

4.1 Year-End Carry-Forward
- On December 31st of each calendar year, HRMS automatically calculates unused AL balances.
- Maximum 10 days of unused Annual Leave (under Policy v2.0) will automatically carry forward to the next year.
- Any unused AL balance exceeding 10 days will lapse automatically unless an explicit exception is granted by the COO (Meera Reddy, EMP-1080).
- Unused SL and CL balances lapse completely on December 31st and cannot be carried forward.

4.2 Leave Encashment Rules
- Annual Leave encashment is permitted ONLY at the time of employee separation (resignation, retirement, or redundancy).
- Encashment Calculation Formula:
Encashment Payout = (Basic Monthly Salary / 26) * Encashable AL Balance (Capped at 30 Days)
- Sick Leave and Casual Leave are strictly non-encashable under all circumstances.

5. ATTENDANCE, PUNCTUALITY, AND WORK FROM HOME (WFH) RULES

5.1 Attendance Capture Systems
- In-Office Tracking: Captured via biometric turnstiles at office entry points.
- WFH Tracking: Captured via automated VPN authentication logs and daily HRMS check-in prompts between 9:00 AM and 9:45 AM.

5.2 Late Arrival & Early Departure Penalties
- Standard Start Time: 9:00 AM. Grace period provided until 9:45 AM.
- Late Arrival Trigger: Clocking in or logging on after 9:45 AM is marked as a "Late Arrival".
- Monthly Penalty Rule:
* 1st & 2nd Late Arrival in a month: Automated HRMS email warning.
* 3rd Late Arrival in a month: Automatic deduction of 0.5 days of Casual Leave (CL).
* 4th and subsequent Late Arrivals: Automatic deduction of 0.5 days of Annual Leave (AL) per instance. If AL is exhausted, 0.5 day LWP salary deduction applies.
- Early Departure Trigger: Logging off or leaving the office before 5:00 PM without prior manager approval results in a half-day leave deduction.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS & FLOATING HOLIDAYS

Nexora observes 10 statutory regional public holidays per calendar year (published every December for the upcoming year for each office location) plus 2 Floating Holidays per employee.
- Floating Holidays must be applied for at least 3 days in advance via HRMS and approved by the manager.
