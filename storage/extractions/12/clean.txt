1. END-TO-END SALES PROCESS & LEAD QUALIFICATION

1.1 The BANT Qualification Framework
All inbound and outbound sales leads generated by Marketing Manager Aditi Bhatia (EMP-1280) must be qualified by Account Executives (e.g., John D'Souza, EMP-1250) using the BANT framework prior to scheduling a technical demo:
- Budget: Does the prospect have approved funding exceeding our minimum subscription tier ($1,200/mo)?
- Authority: Is the primary stakeholder a C-Level Executive (CIO, CISO, CTO) or VP of Engineering/IT?
- Need: Does the prospect require multi-cloud management, zero-trust security, or enterprise LLM deployment?
- Timeline: Is the target deployment window within the next 3 to 6 months?
