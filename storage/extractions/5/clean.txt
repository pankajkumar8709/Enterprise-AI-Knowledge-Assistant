# 1. Enterprise Overview and Organizational Structure
ApexNova Technologies is a fictional enterprise software company that provides workflow automation, analytics, and secure API services to business customers. Its primary production platform is NovaCore, a multi-tenant SaaS platform deployed in the United States and European Union regions.

Atlas Data Lake: analytical storage platform. Owner: Data Platform. Production customer data is encrypted at rest.
PulseDesk: internal support and incident-management platform. Owner: Customer Operations.
LedgerOne: finance and billing system. Owner: Finance. Access requires manager approval and finance-system role assignment.
## 1.3 Decision and escalation model
Operational decisions are delegated to service owners within approved risk limits. Security exceptions require approval from the CISO. Production changes classified as high risk require approval from both the Engineering change owner and Information Security. Customer-impacting incidents are coordinated through PulseDesk and the Incident Commander.
# 2. Information Security and Access Control Policy
## 2.1 Identity and authentication
All employees and contractors must use Orion Identity for corporate authentication. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for administrator accounts, production access, finance systems, and remote administrative sessions. Shared administrator accounts are prohibited.
Access is granted according to least privilege and business need. A manager must approve an employee's initial access request. Access to production systems additionally requires approval from the relevant system owner.
## 2.2 Access review and termination
System owners must review privileged access every 30 days and standard application access every 90 days. People Operations must notify Information Security of an employee termination before the termination becomes effective whenever operationally possible. Corporate identity access must be disabled no later than 15 minutes after an involuntary termination is confirmed.
## 2.3 Data classification
Classification | Examples | Required Control
Public | Published product documentation, press releases | Integrity protection
Internal | Internal procedures, architecture diagrams | Authenticated access
Confidential | Customer contracts, employee records | Encryption and need-to-know access
Restricted | Production credentials, payment data, security secrets | Strong encryption, MFA, strict access logging
## 2.4 Security incident response
Employees must report suspected security incidents to the Security Operations channel in PulseDesk immediately and no later than 30 minutes after discovery. The on-call security engineer assigns an incident severity. Severity 1 incidents require an Incident Commander and executive notification within 30 minutes of confirmation.
Security evidence related to a confirmed incident must be preserved for at least 180 days unless Legal requires a longer period.
# 3. Data Governance, Retention, and Privacy
## 3.1 Data ownership
Every production dataset must have a named business owner and a technical custodian. The business owner determines the permitted business purpose. The technical custodian is responsible for implementation of storage, backup, access-control, and deletion mechanisms.
Data Type | System | Owner | Retention
Customer support tickets | PulseDesk | Customer Operations | 24 months after closure
Customer analytics events | Atlas Data Lake | Data Platform | 13 months
Employee records | People systems | People Operations | 7 years after employment ends, subject to legal requirements
Security audit logs | Atlas Data Lake | Information Security | 180 days
Billing records | LedgerOne | Finance | 7 years
## 3.2 Data deletion
Deletion requests must be recorded in the data-governance queue. The responsible system owner must identify active copies, backups, derived datasets, and downstream exports. A deletion operation is complete only after the owner records the completion date and evidence reference.
## 3.3 Privacy controls
Personal data may be processed only for a documented business purpose.
Exports containing Confidential or Restricted data must use an approved encrypted transfer mechanism.
Customer data must not be copied to personal devices or unmanaged storage.
Production datasets used for development or testing must be masked or replaced with approved synthetic data unless a documented exception exists.
## 3.4 Third-party data processors
New processors handling customer Confidential or Restricted data require Information Security and Legal review before production use. The vendor record must include the processor name, data categories, processing purpose, region, contract status, and accountable business owner.
# 4. Engineering, Change Management, and Service Operations
## 4.1 Production deployment policy
All production deployments to NovaCore must use the approved CI/CD pipeline. Direct changes to production hosts are prohibited except under an approved emergency procedure. Every deployment must have a change record, automated test result, deployment owner, rollback plan, and monitoring confirmation.
## 4.2 Change classification
Change Class | Approval | Minimum Notice
Standard | Service owner | No additional notice when pre-approved
Normal | Service owner + change reviewer | At least 4 business hours
High Risk | Engineering owner + Information Security | At least 24 hours
Emergency | Incident Commander; retrospective review | Immediate, with review within 1 business day
## 4.3 Reliability and service levels
NovaCore has a monthly availability target of 99.95%. The Platform Reliability team owns monitoring, alerting, capacity planning, and disaster-recovery exercises. A Severity 1 service outage requires customer-impact assessment within 15 minutes and an executive status update at least every 30 minutes until stabilization.
## 4.4 Backup and disaster recovery
Critical production databases are backed up daily, with encrypted backups retained for 35 days. A quarterly disaster-recovery exercise must verify restoration of a representative production workload. The target Recovery Point Objective (RPO) for NovaCore transactional data is 24 hours, and the target Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 4 hours.
## 4.5 Engineering security
Dependencies must be scanned for known vulnerabilities before production release. Critical vulnerabilities in production dependencies require remediation or an approved risk exception within 7 calendar days. Secrets must be stored in the approved secrets-management system and must never be committed to source control.
# 5. Support, Procurement, and Enterprise Decision Rules
## 5.1 Customer support
PulseDesk is the system of record for customer support. Customer-impacting Severity 1 tickets must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. Severity 2 tickets must be acknowledged within 2 business hours. Support agents must not request passwords, private keys, MFA codes, or other authentication secrets from customers.
Severity | Example | Initial Response Target
S1 | Production outage affecting multiple customers | 15 minutes
S2 | Major feature unavailable with workaround | 2 business hours
S3 | Normal product defect or degraded behavior | 1 business day
S4 | Question, documentation request, minor issue | 2 business days
## 5.2 Procurement and vendor risk
Purchases above INR 500,000 require Finance approval. Purchases involving customer Restricted data additionally require Information Security review. A vendor with access to production customer data must have a current security assessment and an approved contract before access is provisioned.
## 5.3 Enterprise approval matrix
Decision | Required Approver | Escalation
Production access | System Owner + Manager | CISO for exception
High-risk production change | Engineering Owner + Security | CTO for unresolved conflict
New customer-data processor | Business Owner + Security + Legal | CISO and General Counsel
Purchase > INR 500,000 | Finance | CFO
Security policy exception | CISO | Executive Risk Committee
## 5.4 Key contacts and ownership
Security incidents: Security Operations via PulseDesk; Incident Commander coordinates Severity 1 response.
Production reliability: Platform Reliability team; NovaCore service owner is Engineering.
Privacy and data governance: Data Platform and Legal jointly review high-risk processing.
Employee access and termination: People Operations with Information Security.
Financial controls: Finance; LedgerOne owner is Finance.
