1. RECRUITMENT AND ONBOARDING

1.1 Candidate Selection & Sourcing
All hiring across Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. must be initiated via an approved Requisition Form on the HRMS. Talent Acquisition (Lead: Maya Lin, EMP-1120) manages candidate sourcing. All offers require sign-off from the Department Head and HR Head Rahul Desai (EMP-1001).

1.2 Background Verification (BGV)
Every incoming employee undergoes mandatory third-party Background Verification (BGV) covering:
- Educational degree authenticity.
- Last 5 years of employment verification.
- Criminal background search and identity verification.
- Any falsification of records discovered during BGV results in immediate revocation of the offer or summary termination without notice.

2. PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION PROCESS

2.1 Probationary Period
- Standard Duration: All newly hired employees undergo a mandatory 6-month probation period starting from their official joining date.
- Review Touchpoints: Direct managers must conduct formal documented performance check-ins at 30 days, 90 days, and 150 days.

2.2 Confirmation Evaluation Workflow
At day 150 of probation, the HRMS triggers a Confirmation Review Form to the direct manager.
- Option A: Confirmation - Recommended if employee meets or exceeds performance standards. Upon sign-off by Department Head, a Confirmation Letter is issued. Notice period changes from 15 days to 60 days.
- Option B: Extension - Probation may be extended for a maximum of 3 months if performance needs improvement. A written Performance Improvement Agreement must be attached.
- Option C: Separation - If performance is significantly below standards, service is discontinued with 15 days notice (or salary in lieu thereof).

3. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS AND SALARY REVISIONS

3.1 Appraisal Cycle Schedule
- Annual Appraisal Cycle: Conducted annually in March. Evaluates performance across the preceding April 1 to March 31 period.
- Mid-Year Touchpoint: Conducted in September (Focuses on OKR realignment without compensation changes).

3.2 Salary Revisions & Increment Framework
- Eligibility: Employees confirmed on or before December 31 of the preceding year are eligible for annual salary revisions.
- Rating-Linked Increment Bands:
* Rating 5 (Exceeds All Expectations): 15% - 20% Increase
* Rating 4 (Consistently Exceeds): 10% - 14% Increase
* Rating 3 (Meets Expectations): 5% - 9% Increase
* Rating 1 & 2 (Needs Improvement): 0% (Ineligible for increment)

4. PROMOTIONS & INTERNAL MOBILITY

4.1 Promotion Criteria
Promotions are evaluated during the March annual appraisal cycle. Eligibility requirements:
- Minimum Tenure: 18 months in the current designation/grade.
- Performance Track Record: Rating of 4 or 5 in the latest annual appraisal, and no rating below 3 in the preceding 2 years.
- Executive Approval: Approved by Department Head and Head of HR (Rahul Desai, EMP-1001).

4.2 Internal Job Posting (IJP) & Mobility Rules
- Eligibility: Employees who have completed 18 months in their current role may apply for internal openings published on the IJP portal.
- Application Flow: Employee submits application via IJP portal -> Automated alert sent to current manager -> Interview conducted by hiring manager -> If selected, transition occurs within 30 calendar days.

5. EMPLOYEE RECOGNITION & REWARDS

5.1 Monthly & Quarterly Awards
- Spot Award: Cash prize of ₹10,000 / SGD $200 awarded by managers for exceptional project delivery.
- Star of the Quarter: Awarded at the department level with a trophy and cash prize of ₹50,000 / SGD $800.

6. DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES & PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS (PIP)

6.1 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Mechanics
- Trigger: Assigned when an employee receives a Rating 1 or 2 during performance reviews, or displays persistent performance failure.
- Duration: Exactly 60 calendar days.
- Process: Direct manager drafts a weekly milestone plan reviewed by HR Business Partner Aisha Khan (EMP-1402). Weekly progress reviews are documented in HRMS.
- PIP Outcomes:
* Pass: Employee achieves target metrics and resumes regular status.
* Fail: Employee service is terminated for cause under poor performance grounds with 30 days severance pay.

7. RESIGNATION, TERMINATION, AND SEPARATION

7.1 Separation Categories
- Voluntary Resignation: Employee initiates exit via HRMS. 60-day notice served.
- Involuntary Termination for Cause: Initiated by company due to gross misconduct, POSH breach, fraud, or security violation. Zero notice period, zero severance.
- Termination for Performance: Initiated post PIP failure with 30 days notice or pay in lieu.

7.2 Exit Interview & Full & Final (F&F) Settlement
- Exit Interview: Mandatory session conducted by HRBP within the final 5 days of notice period to gather institutional feedback.
- Asset Offboarding: IT access revoked at 6:00 PM on the Last Working Day (LWD). Laptops returned to IT Head Amit Patel (EMP-1015).
- F&F Payment: Disbursed by Senior Payroll Manager Arjun Nair (EMP-1134) within 45 days of LWD.
