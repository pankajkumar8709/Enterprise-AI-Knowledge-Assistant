SECTION 1: VENDOR ONBOARDING & RISK EVALUATION

1.1 Vendor Registration Workflow
All external vendors providing hardware, software, cloud infrastructure, or consulting services to Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. must undergo formal registration managed by Vendor Risk Specialist Ramesh Babu (EMP-1499).

1.2 Vendor Due Diligence & Security Vetting
Before issuing a Purchase Order (PO), the vendor must submit:
- Corporate Registration & Tax Identification (PAN, GSTIN in India; UEN in Singapore).
- SOC 2 Type II Report or ISO 27001 Certification (Mandatory for all SaaS/Cloud software vendors).
- Cybersecurity Review Clearance: Evaluated and signed off by CISO Karthik Natarajan (EMP-1072) to ensure compliance with Zero-Trust guidelines.
- Anti-Bribery & Corruption Declaration: In compliance with the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and local anti-corruption laws, vetted by General Counsel Neha Gupta (EMP-1065).

SECTION 3: PURCHASE REQUEST (PR) TO PURCHASE ORDER (PO) WORKFLOW

1. Requisition Initiation: Requester creates a Purchase Request (PR) in the Procurement System specifying business justification, department allocation code, and budget availability.
2. Sourcing Review: Procurement Specialist Ramesh Babu verifies vendor qualifications and quotation comparison matrix.
3. PO Generation: Upon reaching required approval thresholds, Head of Procurement Rajesh Khanna (EMP-1099) issues the official binding Purchase Order (PO).
4. Goods Received Note (GRN): Upon delivery of hardware/services, IT Head Amit Patel (EMP-1015) or requesting manager signs off the GRN in the portal.
5. Invoice Matching & Payment: Senior Accountant Deepa Joshi (EMP-1135) performs 3-way matching (PO + GRN + Vendor Invoice). Standard Payment Terms: Net 45 Days from invoice receipt date.

SECTION 4: EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT PROTOCOL

In the event of a Severity 0 / Severity 1 system outage, data center hardware failure, or active security breach:
- Emergency Override: Head of IT Amit Patel (EMP-1015) or CISO Karthik Natarajan (EMP-1072) may bypass the 3-quote waiting period to procure critical hardware, software patches, or external forensic incident response services.
- Verbal Sign-off: Requires verbal approval from COO Meera Reddy (EMP-1080) or CFO Sneha Iyer (EMP-1022).
- Retroactive Documentation: Formal Purchase Requisition and retroactive PO must be generated within 48 hours post-incident containment.
